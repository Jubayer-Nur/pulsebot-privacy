PulseBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. About

PulseBot provides AI-powered customer service automation for Facebook Pages (the "Service"). This policy explains how we handle data when you use our Service.

2. Data We Collect

  • Facebook Page IDs and Page Access Tokens (authentication)
  • Message content from Messenger conversations and Page comments (to generate AI responses)
  • Basic usage logs (timestamps, error messages)

3. How We Use Data

We use collected data solely to generate AI responses on behalf of the Facebook Page owner. We do not sell or share data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage

Messages are processed in real-time. Debug logs are retained for up to 30 days and then deleted.

5. Third-Party Services

AI responses are generated using OpenRouter and Google Gemini. Message delivery uses Meta's Graph API. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, email us at the address below. We will process requests within 7 days.
